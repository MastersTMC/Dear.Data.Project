Processing rules of the art:

* Size driven (the larger the stone the wider the radius of the ellipse)
* Texture driven (the smoother it is the higher it is in y axis) the list as follows: smooth, round, waxy, grainy, rough, jagged, hard.
* Color driven (each color will be given a specific rgb color and applied based on the color the stone is)
* Days driven and the day it was found (as the list goes by, the stones would be in order from 1-100 or from left to right)

&#x20;

\-declare a format of 12 colors, each color represents rgb. 

\-y axis from 1-8 (hard-smooth), where each 1-8 is at a random correlation to prevent straight lines.

\-x axis from list 1-100.

\-the larger the size of the stone, the larger the ellipse's radius (1cm = 2pixels, 10cm = 20pixels)
